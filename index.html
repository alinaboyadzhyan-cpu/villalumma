About the Villa

An award-winning Mediterranean lifestyle

Nestled between the Mediterranean Sea and the green hills of Finestrat, Villa Lumma stands as a symbol of refined coastal living — surrounded by internationally acclaimed golf courses and framed by an elegant balance of architecture, nature and serenity.

This prestigious enclave on Spain's Costa Blanca is celebrated for its year-round sunshine, world-class leisure facilities and exclusive privacy. A home at Villa Lumma means embracing a sophisticated Mediterranean lifestyle, where every day blends beauty, comfort and timeless elegance.
